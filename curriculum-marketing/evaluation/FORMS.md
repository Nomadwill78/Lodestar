# Evaluation Forms A–D

Full instrument text. Paste directly into a print template or Google Forms.

`[MODULE]` and the four `[OBJECTIVE n]` slots are filled from `ITEM-BANK.md`
for the module being delivered. Everything else is identical across all 15
modules — build it once.

Design conventions used throughout: the 1–5 anchors never change, no item is
required, and every open field is optional. See `README.md` §4 for scoring.

### §1 — What changed

For each skill, give **two** ratings: where you are now, and — looking back with
what you know now — where you actually were before today.

The second column is not a trick. People routinely discover during a session that
a skill involved more than they realized, which means this morning's self-rating
was made with a different yardstick. Rating both now, on the same yardstick, gives
us a truer picture.

```
1 = Not at all   2 = A little   3 = Somewhat   4 = Well   5 = Very well
```
