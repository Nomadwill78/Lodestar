# Self-Efficacy Item Bank — All 15 Modules

Four items per module, one per learning objective, written as capability
statements. Paste the matching set into §1 of Form A and §1 of Form B.

**Sourcing note.** For 11 modules the items are derived from the `Learning
objectives` block stated verbatim in the module's facilitator guide. For 4
modules — marked **§** below — the guide file would not open, so items are
derived from the four section titles and section content in the module's slide
deck. The catalog is consistent (four sections, four objectives, one per
section), so these are sound, but check them against the guide's own objectives
block before first use and correct any drift.

Items are worded so a participant can answer honestly on arrival without having
seen the content — a person who cannot do the thing should be able to tell that
they cannot. Avoid jargon introduced during the session, or the pre-rating
measures vocabulary rather than capability.

## Leadership and Management Track

### LM-01 · Foundations of People Management

1. I can describe what my job as a manager is measured on, as distinct from doing the work myself.
2. I can work out where a team member stands on a specific task, and adjust how I lead them to match.
3. I can hand off a piece of work with clear decision rights and agreed checkpoints, then follow up without micromanaging.
4. I can run a one-on-one that belongs to my team member, and give feedback specific enough to act on.

### LM-02 · Emotionally Intelligent Leadership

1. I can name the domains of emotional intelligence and identify my own strongest and weakest.
2. I can use a specific technique to steady myself in a high-stress or high-conflict moment.
3. I can check my read of a team member's emotional state instead of acting on my assumption.
4. I can build a development plan grounded in feedback from other people, not just my own reflection.

### LM-03 · Leading Through Change and Transition

1. I can tell the difference between the external change my organization is making and the internal transition my team is going through.
2. I can sequence a change effort through clear stages and identify which stage we have skipped.
3. I can make a case for change that names what people are losing as well as what they gain.
4. I can build a stakeholder map and a communication plan for a real initiative.

### LM-04 · Coaching for Performance and Growth

1. I can run a structured coaching conversation that moves someone from a goal to committed action.
2. I can stay in open questions rather than supplying the answer when someone brings me a problem.
3. I can give feedback that stays on behavior, paired with a clear standard and genuine belief in the person.
4. I can set up a check-in structure that supports follow-through rather than policing it.

### LM-05 · Difficult Conversations and Conflict Resolution

1. I can prepare for a high-stakes conversation by separating what I observed from the story I built about it.
2. I can name my default conflict style and deliberately choose a different one when the situation calls for it.
3. I can notice when a conversation has stopped being a real dialogue, and do something specific to bring it back.
4. I can move a conversation from stated positions to underlying interests and reach an agreement that holds.

### LM-06 · Inclusive Leadership and Cultural Humility

1. I can treat cultural humility as an ongoing practice, including how I respond when I get something wrong.
2. I can identify where bias is most likely to enter my everyday decisions about hiring, assignments, credit, and feedback.
3. I can use specific behaviors that make it safer for people on my team to speak up and disagree.
4. I can redesign a real team process so it relies on structure rather than on my good intentions.

### LM-07 · Delegation, Prioritization, and Accountability

1. I can decide which work to hand off, and match it to someone's capability and growth.
2. I can sort competing demands by what matters most rather than by whichever deadline is loudest.
3. I can hand off work with one named owner, explicit decision rights, and agreed checkpoints.
4. I can design follow-up that lands as support rather than surveillance.

## Nonprofit Management and Capacity Track

### NM-01 · Board Governance Essentials

1. I can describe the duties of care, loyalty, and obedience in my own words.
2. I can tell whether a specific decision belongs to the board or to staff.
3. I can identify concrete gaps in how our board recruits, orients, and engages its members.
4. I can place our organization on its lifecycle and name the governance approach that fits it.

### NM-02 · Fundraising and Donor Development

1. I can map where a real donor sits on their journey with us, and name what they need at that stage.
2. I can write a case for support that centers the community's dignity rather than its deficits.
3. I can assess how concentrated our revenue is and name a stream worth growing.
4. I can design stewardship touchpoints matched to a donor's actual stage rather than one generic sequence.

### NM-03 · Grant Writing and Grants Management

1. I can qualify a funder before writing anything, and decide honestly whether to pursue or pass.
2. I can build a logic model and tell the difference between an output and an outcome.
3. I can build a budget where every line maps to a described activity, including a justified indirect rate.
4. I can set up a tracking rhythm that keeps our reporting timely, complete, and honest.

### NM-04 · Program Design and Evaluation

1. I can write an if-then pathway for a real program and name its riskiest assumption.
2. I can choose an indicator that is both meaningful and realistically measurable with our capacity.
3. I can design data collection that limits the burden on the community and shares findings back.
4. I can name who will use an evaluation, and for what decision, before designing it.

### NM-05 · Financial Management for Nonprofit Leaders **§**

1. I can read our three core financial statements and say what question each one answers.
2. I can explain the difference between restricted and unrestricted funds, and estimate a program's true full cost.
3. I can calculate our months of unrestricted cash on hand and how concentrated our revenue is.
4. I can audit our internal controls and name the segregation-of-duties gap we most need to close.

### NM-06 · Volunteer Engagement and Management **§**

1. I can write a volunteer role description tied to a real organizational need, with purpose, time commitment, and support.
2. I can audit our recruitment channels and name who they currently miss.
3. I can match recognition to what actually motivates a specific volunteer.
4. I can set screening proportional to a role's real risk, and hold a respectful redirection conversation.

### NM-07 · Strategic Planning for Mission-Driven Organizations **§**

1. I can run a simple, inclusive environmental scan that surfaces uncomfortable truths, not just confirmation.
2. I can narrow to three to five priorities against clear criteria, and name what we are deliberately setting aside.
3. I can turn a priority into a specific goal with one named owner and a measure we can realistically track.
4. I can set a review cadence with real dates and a named convener before the planning session ends.

### NM-08 · Building a Culture of Equity and Belonging **§**

1. I can examine a policy at all three layers: what is written, how it is actually applied, and the informal culture around it.
2. I can design a listening structure that gives affected people real influence and visibly closes the loop.
3. I can draft specific, observable team norms and name who is responsible for upholding them.
4. I can design an equity measure with a named owner and a review cadence, respecting privacy and sample-size limits.

## Notes for whoever maintains this

- **Keep the wording frozen once you start collecting.** Changing an item's
  wording breaks comparability with everything gathered before it. If an item
  must change, treat it as a new item and note the delivery it changed at.
- **Cut items that produce no variance.** If everyone rates an item 5 on both
  pre and post across two deliveries, it is measuring nothing. Replace it.
- **These four items are also a sales asset.** They are the clearest short
  statement of what a buyer's people will be able to do afterward — reusable
  almost verbatim in the course catalog (item P3) and in proposals.
- **Cross-track duplication is real and fine.** LM-06 and NM-08 both address
  equity; LM-01, LM-04, and LM-07 all touch delegation and feedback. Where a
  participant takes both, expect a higher pre-rating on the second — worth
  noting in any report rather than reading it as a weaker workshop.
